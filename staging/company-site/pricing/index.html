Common questions

Know the terms
before you start.

These describe the intended offer. This preview does not accept signups or payments.

Does the trial require a card?

Yes. The intended offer is a 14-day trial with a valid payment card required through secure Stripe Checkout. Email verification alone does not start the trial.

What is charged at the start?

The planned trial starts at £0. After 14 days, the subscription becomes £99 per month unless cancelled.

When would the 14 days begin?

The trial clock starts only after the completed subscription Checkout has been verified. A page redirect alone does not confirm activation.

Can I cancel before being charged?

The planned offer allows cancellation before the trial ends to avoid the recurring subscription. There is no minimum term.

Can I start a trial here?

Trial enrolment and Checkout are not enabled in this design preview. Launch testing and release approval remain outstanding.

Where can I explore Route Operations Dashboard first?

Use the Route Operations Dashboard page for the overview video and real application screens, or open the complete 27-page User Guide.
