Project snapshot

RSMCR Logistics

UK courier & delivery operations

  • Business website and visual branding
  • Driver-payment worksheet improvements
  • Ongoing website maintenance

A practical client project, separate from our Route Operations Dashboard product.

The starting point

A straightforward question.
How can we make this easier?

Daily worksheets, carrier information and driver payments all need attention, even when there are more pressing jobs to manage.

With new contracts bringing different payment rates, RSMCR wanted practical support for the administration alongside its core delivery work.

That conversation helped shape a simple principle for the project: focus on the problems people actually encounter during a working week.

What we worked on

Useful improvements.
Without unnecessary complexity.

Support across the public-facing business website and the spreadsheets used for everyday driver-payment administration.

01 · DIGITAL PRESENCE

A clearer business website

Designed and built RSMCR's website and visual presentation to communicate its logistics services in a professional, consistent way.

02 · EVERYDAY ADMIN

More consistent calculations

Improved existing driver-payment worksheets, including calculations based on agreed drop bands and payment rates.

03 · CONTINUING SUPPORT

Keeping the site maintained

Provide ongoing website maintenance and agreed updates as RSMCR's needs change.

What matters to us

Listen to the operation.
Then build for it.

This kind of hands-on client work keeps our thinking grounded in real administration rather than hypothetical use cases.

It also informs our wider research into practical tools for courier operations, driver pay and reporting.

For clarity: this client story covers RSMCR's website and spreadsheet support. It is not a claim that RSMCR uses Fashearn Labs Route Operations Dashboard v1.1, which remains in launch testing.
