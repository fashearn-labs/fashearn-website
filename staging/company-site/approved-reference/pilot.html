Pilot process

Enquire. Review.
Guided setup.

  1. 01 · ENQUIRE

    Tell us about your business

    Submit the existing Dashboard enquiry form, including approximate drivers and carriers.

  2. 02 · REVIEW

    We assess your request

    We check suitability and readiness; not every enquiry guarantees a pilot place.

  3. 03 · ARRANGE ACCESS

    Receive your setup key

    Once approved and ready, we'll send secure browser access instructions and a unique setup key.

  4. 04 · EVALUATE

    Start the agreed pilot

    Complete the first-time setup wizard and use the application under agreed pilot terms.
