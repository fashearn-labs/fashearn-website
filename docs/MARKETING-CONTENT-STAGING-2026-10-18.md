# Five checks before approving a courier driver pay run

A weekly driver pay run may draw on several sources: the daily work record, carrier/contract rates, attendance information and operational exceptions. Before locking the results, a consistent review is more useful than a collection of untracked adjustments.

1. **Check the work period and drivers.** Confirm every included shift or day belongs to the right pay period and driver, without duplicates.
2. **Check the agreed rate basis.** Different delivery contracts use different rules. Review the actual applicable rate rather than assuming every route or stop pays alike.
3. **Resolve exceptions before approval.** Missing work, disputed counts or manual corrections should have a visible reason and an owner for the decision.
4. **Review draft totals against supporting records.** Make sure the totals can be explained and that the original sources remain available.
5. **Record the approval.** Keep the approved status, supporting adjustments and an Audit Trail so future questions can be reviewed without silently rewriting the approved pay run.
