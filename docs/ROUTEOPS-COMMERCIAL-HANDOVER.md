## Product direction

RouteOps Core is a finished operational software product for delivery contractors and multi-carrier operators.

The commercial direction is product-first rather than bespoke consultancy. The aim is to minimise manual contact and support overhead by offering a standardised, repeatable system with a clear trial, fixed monthly price and self-explanatory product presentation.

Long-term direction:
- increasingly automated acquisition, trial and onboarding
- minimal bespoke development
- low-contact customer handling
- repeatable product delivery
- continued product improvement from real operational use
- preserve a clean GitHub history for product, website, media and commercial changes

## Core product positioning

RouteOps brings the following into one controlled workflow:
- driver pay
- carrier reconciliation
- exception control
- reporting
- profitability / commercial visibility

It is positioned as an alternative to disconnected spreadsheets and repetitive manual administration.
