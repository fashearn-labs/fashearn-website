## Style rules

The final guide should be:
- simple and screenshot-led
- one task or concept per page/section
- numbered in strict order
- low on jargon
- explicit about where to click
- clear about actions that lock, approve or finalise data
- suitable for self-service onboarding with minimal support

Guide callouts:
- **STEP** - action to take
- **CHECK** - confirm the screen or result before continuing
- **STOP** - do not continue until the warning is understood/resolved

## Page 1 - Welcome / Getting Started

### Intro

RouteOps Core brings driver pay, reconciliation, exceptions, reporting and profitability into one controlled workflow. This guide is written for first-time users and assumes no technical knowledge.

### Before you begin

- **Your RouteOps login** - Use the login details supplied for your account.
- **Your operating data** - Have driver/carrier data ready when you reach the import section.
- **A few quiet minutes** - Follow the guide in order the first time; do not rush Pay Run steps.

### How this guide works

- **STEP - Do this:** Follow the numbered actions exactly in order.
- **CHECK - Check this:** Confirm your screen matches before moving on.
- **STOP - Do not continue:** Warnings appear before actions that lock or finalise data.
