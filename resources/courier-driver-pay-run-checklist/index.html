Five checks before approving a courier driver pay run

A driver pay run can bring together daily work records, attendance, agreed pay rates and delivery exceptions. Before approving the figures, follow the same checks each time so that payments and adjustments can be explained.

  1. 01

    Confirm the pay period and drivers.

    Check that each recorded day or shift belongs to the correct driver and pay period. Look for missing or duplicate entries.

  2. 02

    Verify the agreed pay basis.

    Check the rate and calculation method for each contract. Pay might be based on shifts, routes, stops or another agreed measure; do not assume that all contracts work the same way.

  3. 03

    Review exceptions and adjustments.

    Check missing work, disputed counts and manual changes. Record the explanation, supporting information and decision maker. Do not approve a disputed figure until it has been reviewed.

  4. 04

    Compare draft totals with supporting records.

    Check the proposed payments against the work records, applicable rates and adjustments. Keep the sources available for later questions.

  5. 05

    Record the approval.

    Record who approved the pay run and when. Retain the approved status, supporting adjustments and a usable Audit Trail so subsequent questions can be reviewed.
